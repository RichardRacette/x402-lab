# Role Reality Check V0 — INVALIDATED / HISTORICAL

Status: **do not implement as a paid automated product**

Date: **2026-08-24**

This contract was prepared as a validation candidate and was invalidated by deeper competitor/profitability research before implementation.

The key finding was that current free/SMB recruiting-intelligence products already provide materially richer role-level market intelligence than this proposed V0, while recruiting calibration agents and public-data DIY workflows are also emerging rapidly.

The project therefore rejected the planned `$0.50` automated Role Reality endpoint rather than turning a clean specification into a sunk-cost trap.

Concepts from this document — occupation normalization, compensation context, source provenance, careful handling of missing data and calibration questions — may still be reused **inside the internal Search Preflight Workbench** where they reduce human fulfillment time.

They do not constitute an approved customer-facing contract.
