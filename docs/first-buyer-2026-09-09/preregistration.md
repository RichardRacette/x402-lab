## Fixed buyer task and competitors

A CRM operator must route submitted contacts to KEEP, REVIEW, or BLOCK. KEEP means
no demonstrated reason to reject at this layer; it does NOT establish mailbox
existence or consent to contact. Disposable membership is REVIEW, not an automatic
ban. DNS uncertainty and unsupported valid formats must remain REVIEW. Invalid
syntax and definitive no-mail routing can justify BLOCK. Do not send any email.

Compare the smallest candidate core against maintained `python-email-validator`,
`dnspython`, and the same pinned disposable-domain list. Both get identical DNS
fixtures, including explicit failure states, and comparable implementation budgets
(maximum 30 minutes each). The reviewer owns 20 fixed cases: 14 public and six held
out from the candidate implementer. Expected routes are fixed first. Protocol
conformance and routing semantics, not identical JSON or seller-specific formatting,
determine completion.

The no-build path is to keep the current intake workflow and review uncertain
contacts, or adopt the free local validator if the organization can run Python.
Already-owned CRM/email-vendor validation and plan allowances have $0 incremental
cash cost within quota. A runtime restricted to HTTP must also consider free public
DNS-over-HTTPS and an existing vendor, rather than being artificially denied tools.

## Predeclared seller gate

Build a seller only if ALL hold:

- At least 19/20 cases complete correctly, with zero unjustified definitive BLOCK
  decisions and zero unsupported mailbox-existence claims.
- A material advantage: at least four additional correct cases over the strongest
  realistic substitute, OR at least 50% measured integration-time reduction at
  equivalent correctness. A difference in developer language familiarity is not
  evidence of customer integration savings.
- No material supply, privacy, freshness, reliability, or cost compromise. Public
  DNS is not exclusive data, and local processing avoids disclosure of full contacts.
- Total buyer cost is competitive after integration/maintenance, retries/failures,
  existing allowances, and payment overhead. Unknown integration or operator costs
  cannot be set to zero to claim a win.

If substitute already meets the task and the candidate cannot demonstrate this
advantage, return NO_BUILD for this sprint. If comparison is prevented by access,
report INCONCLUSIVE and the smallest missing evidence; do not build a seller.
