# First-buyer sprint: bounded buyer and competition evidence

Research date: 2026-09-09. Scope: the three candidates below, plus the generic-wrapper control and no-build. Primary product, documentation, open-source, and first-person buyer sources were read; x402scan figures are explicitly provider-reported directory observations. No signup, outreach, purchase, live wallet inspection, endpoint invocation, or deployment was performed.

## Decision supplied to the sprint lead

**A qualifies only for one cheap, local comparison, not a seller build.** There is an actual recurring CRM hygiene problem and adjacent spending, so the narrow hypothesis that an HTTP-constrained, low-volume agent operator values managed triage deserves falsification. x402-lab's reliability, maintenance, integration-time, and total-cost advantages are all **unproven**. B is already supplied by Apify itself. C overlaps both paid specialists and free tools without a demonstrated x402-lab advantage. If A cannot materially beat a properly equipped substitute under the predeclared criteria, stop with `NO_BUILD` and redirect the next implementation session to Talent Bench's first observed recruiter rediscovery session using the existing synthetic kit and frozen V0.1 scope.

This conclusion follows the product thesis's buy-versus-build, intelligence-abundance, positive-sum, and ownership gates. The owner's current sprint instruction permits a bounded local experiment beyond the old discovery-only milestone; it does not turn that prototype into commercial validation. The 2026-08-24 Observatory and Issue #39 remain historical observations, not current proof of independent purchasers. Buyer Trace is separate.

## Card A — Email-domain triage before agent-driven CRM intake

**Provisional experiment only.** The payer is a small team's owner or revenue-operations lead funding an agent that repeatedly prepares inbound contacts for CRM intake. The narrow task is to identify malformed addresses, unusable domains, and domains appearing on a maintained disposable-domain list while preserving unresolved cases for human policy or later evidence. It is not automated outbound prospecting or guaranteed deliverability.

**Exact output hypothesized:** one machine-readable record per address with normalized address/domain, syntax assessment, observed DNS routing evidence, source/time for disposable-list matches, timestamps/expiry, explicit unavailable/unsupported states, and `mailbox_status: unknown`. Domain routability never proves that a mailbox exists. Risk flags should support the buyer's decision; disposable status alone must not silently delete a real contact or claim fraud. A purchased result could save repeated integration and maintenance work in an HTTP-only runtime. That benefit has not been measured.

**Problem and adjacent spend.** On [HubSpot Community, 2023-08-17](https://community.hubspot.com/t/spam-disposable-email-domains-on-form-fills/87714), user SWandell7 reported manually fixing disposable/spam domain form submissions and requested a solution. This is a first-person problem, but old and not an agent buyer. In [Emailable's HubSpot marketplace reviews](https://ecosystem.hubspot.com/marketplace/apps/emailable), a 2025-08-25 reviewer described paying $38 and objected to the minimum credit bundle and integration experience. The vendor's 2025-09-03 reply disputed the support characterization and confirmed the then-current 5,000-credit minimum. Another 2025-03-19 review described integration usefulness and list synchronization delay. These are useful adjacent use/spend signals, not independently verified invoices, willingness to buy our weaker DNS-only result, or present interest. The marketplace's roughly 1K installs includes no demonstrated agent-use breakdown.

Other credible substitutes reinforce the same limit: [Reacher](https://github.com/reacherhq/check-if-email-exists) supplies a self-hosted HTTP/CLI verifier but adds port-25/proxy operation and an AGPL/commercial licensing choice. [A community Apify Actor](https://apify.com/garnet_puppet/email-verifier) explicitly supplies DNS/list triage with unknown mailbox state, showing that honest uncertainty is already a product feature. Its page's no-MX assertion needs scrutiny because implicit A/AAAA fallback exists; do not copy vendor accuracy claims. Neither substitute was executed.

**Supply and operation.** Public DNS and a pinned, permissively usable maintained domain list provide a plausible supply route. They are shared inputs, not proprietary supply. Keep version, retrieval time, upstream attribution, matching rules, and stale behavior explicit. There is no proven contracted DNS SLA or verified live list-refresh guarantee here. Cash cost for a locally downloaded list is zero; hosting, DNS operation, cache refresh, failed lookups, abuse, retries, and customer support are not zero. Per-call cost and price must come from the operating-cost workstream; this research does not measure them. Freshness should be bounded by actual DNS TTL/list age, not an invented universal guarantee. A full address need not be disclosed to a DNS resolver because the query concerns its domain.

**Advantage, distribution, and ownership.** The only credible early hypothesis is less setup/maintenance plus correctly bounded evidence for low-volume operators who lack a local runtime or an already-connected verifier. For developers who can run the baseline, or teams already using Hunter/Emailable/CRM controls, the hypothesis is weak. Existing x402 infrastructure reduces our build effort; that alone does not reduce the buyer's total cost. A route to users is the public CRM automation communities where this problem is reported, then a concrete integration example and relevant directory listing if launch is separately approved. Neither community reach nor directory presence establishes interest. Owned assets could be a tested contract, operational reliability, and opted-in customer relationships; public DNS/list contents do not become exclusive assets.

**Alignment and stop condition.** Buyer value is fewer avoidable intake mistakes and less maintenance; revenue should grow with useful recurring batches rather than confidence inflation or charging repeatedly for unchanged cached facts. The buyer keeps acceptance/deletion authority. Unknowns preserve agency. Stop if a realistic free/already-owned workflow completes the same cases at comparable correctness with modest integration work and lower total cost; if genuine users require mailbox existence; or if the hypothetical runtime constraint cannot be tied to a real buyer. Competition alone is not the stop condition; lack of material advantage is.

**One recommended falsification:** the sprint lead's predeclared approximately 20-case comparison of A against `python-email-validator` plus the same maintained list, with held-out failures/unfamiliar inputs and no-build, measuring task completion/correctness, uncertainty, source freshness, buyer latency, integration effort, and total cost. Allow the buyer reviewer to choose the substitute or no purchase. Treat unavailable paid/live-service comparisons as unavailable. This report supplies qualification evidence only; no measured outcome is claimed here.

## Card B — Credentialless, spend-capped Apify access

**Reject an x402-lab seller for this sprint.** The payer would fund a research/operations agent needing maintained web extraction or other existing Actors without arranging a persistent provider account. The purchased output is an expiring prepaid API token, then metered Actor execution; it is not a data result by itself. The directory observation is adjacent spending on token acquisition, with buyer independence and downstream usefulness unknown.

The decisive substitute is [Apify AGI itself](https://agi.apify.com/): the first party already exchanges x402/MPP payments for spend-capped tokens usable directly against its API and MCP. Current terms say $1 minimum, approximately 14-day lifetime, unused balance non-refundable. [Apify's current integration documentation](https://docs.apify.com/integrations/x402) limits eligible Actors to appropriate event-based billing, limited permissions, and other conditions; not every Store Actor is eligible. Neither a token nor public data visibility grants blanket rights to all data uses.

[Conventional Apify pricing](https://apify.com/pricing) includes a free account with $5 monthly usage and a $19/month Starter plan with prepaid usage plus overages. For an existing account holder, available credit and current API/MCP integration can have zero incremental access cost. The open-source [Crawlee](https://crawlee.dev/) route can supply browser/crawler machinery, with the operator taking on maintenance and hosting; it is not equivalent to every maintained Actor. No new account is authorized here.

Apify owns the supply, token minting, metering, and established distribution. A reseller must fund upstream balances, manage expiry/stranded credits, uncertain runs/refunds, failures, security, and support; lawful resale/contract terms would need specific confirmation. No wholesale discount or permission advantage is established. A wrapper's price has to cover those costs while a buyer can go directly to Apify. Existing x402-lab payment controls do not establish a reason for Apify buyers to change vendors. The disqualifier is already present: the first party removes the exact stated friction. Do not invent a new orchestration product to evade this finding. Use direct Apify when actually needed and separately authorized; do not build this offer.

## Card C — Public agent-readiness / launch checks

**Reject an x402-lab seller for this sprint.** The payer would be a developer team funding an agent that checks a public deployment after changes. Output would be timestamped bounded evidence for discoverability, DNS/HTTPS, machine-facing metadata, schema/protocol availability, and actionable failures. Such observation remains useful when intelligence becomes cheaper, but a general readiness score is not evidence of actual successful agent use.

Directory activity is small and mixed across resources. AgentBodega advertises `/api/audit/agent-launch` at $0.03, five-URL batches at $0.05, and narrower checks at $0.01–$0.02. Santos advertises an $0.08 passive readiness audit and a limited free demo. Their listing totals cannot establish payment for these exact resources. No verified independent current buyer request for an additional x402-lab readiness product was found in this bounded review.

The free/OSS competitive set is material: [Lighthouse CI](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/lighthouse-ci) already automates recurring accessibility, SEO, and performance checks; [openapi-agent-ready](https://github.com/sfaisal/agent-ready) supplies API/MCP-readiness linting and CI gates; [MnT agentready](https://github.com/MnT-Future/agentready) is an MIT website/commerce scanner with JSON and CI output, versioned protocol research, and explicit assessment limits. These are overlapping, not fully identical, substitutes. Existing shell/browser/HTTP tools and a project's CI checks are the already-owned/no-build path. A buyer with an actual gap could combine the relevant checks rather than buy an undifferentiated grade.

Supply is public bounded fetches and published protocol rules; costs include network fetches, browser time if needed, timeouts/redirects, spec drift, false alarms, abuse controls, and explaining partial results. There is no measured uptime/SLA advantage or scarce owned input. Developer communities and package/CI integrations are reachable distribution routes, but x402-lab has no established pipeline there. A concrete buyer-specific regression that existing tools miss could reopen the hypothesis later. The current disqualifier is no demonstrated material workflow advantage, not the mere existence of competitors. Do not broaden this into another scanner or dashboard.

## Generic wrappers as a fair control

The current Judgment & Synthesis listing is no longer zero: 28 displayed calls from one source-defined buyer. It offers researched results, search, scrape, classification, and routing without signup. Live retrieval and bundled access can be useful even when generic reasoning is cheap; a thin wrapper may reduce integration cost. Its concentration and opaque resource attribution prevent concluding broad demand. Conversely, historic zero could reflect low exposure, listing age, missing indexing, or sales elsewhere. A properly equipped generic model plus already-owned search/browser tools belongs in the no-build comparison where it can do the task. Neither this listing nor three selected utility listings can establish a causal market-wide preference for deterministic products.

## Evidence limits and authority

The named public commenters and vendor case-study users are prospects only in the loose research sense; none requested this product or was contacted. No customer willingness, independent agent purchase, retention, contribution margin, or live fulfillment has been demonstrated. No precise economics can be inferred from directory volume. This research proposes no paid intelligence purchase because transaction-level evidence would not resolve the already-visible substitute problem. The next useful action is the bounded local comparison already assigned by the sprint lead. A seller or launch package is warranted only after that comparison passes the owner's stated gate.
