## Why this brief was stopped

This build brief was prepared during Product Discovery Milestone 4.5, then deliberately stopped before implementation when competitive research invalidated its differentiation premise.

Current competitors already cover too much of the proposed surface:

- Reqbeat exposes hiring pulse, repost pain, first-hire signals, a pre-action brief, outcome capture, attribution, and calibrated intent.
- Signalbase sells hiring velocity, role shelf-life, talent-supply strain, repost density, first-hire detection, API/webhooks/MCP, and recruiting-agency workflows.
- Recruitcha already packages fresh/stale roles and related agency-BD intelligence.

Building the original Recruiting Pressure Brief now would violate the project's Product Viability & Profit Gate because the proposed product does not have a strong enough buy-vs-build or differentiation advantage.

## Historical value

The prior version of this file remains in Git history as useful research scaffolding. It helped us specify:

- deterministic signal rules
- truthful history handling
- source provenance
- bounded fulfillment
- preflight-before-payment behavior
- recruiter-facing falsification criteria

Those principles may be reused in a different product, but **the Greenhouse recruiting-pressure endpoint described in the prior version is not approved for Codex implementation.**
