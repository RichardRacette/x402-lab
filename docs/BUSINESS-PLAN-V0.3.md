# Business Plan v0.3 — SUPERSEDED

Status: **historical product-discovery document**

Date: **2026-08-24**

Business Plan v0.3 moved the project from unvalidated automated APIs to a service-first Search Preflight experiment.

A subsequent zero-based review kept the **service-first / productize-what-repeats** principle but found a stronger buyer and product:

> **Recruiting Agent Practitioner Eval — domain-expert human evaluation of recruiting-agent runs, traces and workflows.**

Why v0.3 was superseded:

- many recruiting agents are already deployed for teams with internal recruiter approval, weakening the need for an outside per-requisition reviewer
- domain-expert AI evaluation is a demonstrated paid service category
- recruiting-agent builders have a clearer last-mile need for independent practitioner labels, failure taxonomies and golden/regression cases
- those labels can compound into a benchmark and later automated evaluator instead of disappearing after each service engagement

Search Preflight remains useful as an **evaluation scenario family**, not the primary commercial product.
