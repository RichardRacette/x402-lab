# Business Plan v0.2 — SUPERSEDED

Status: **historical product-discovery document**

Date: **2026-08-24**

This plan centered on an automated **Role Reality Check** as the next possible paid SKU.

A subsequent full profitability audit invalidated that automated product hypothesis after current competitor research found that:

- free/SMB products already provide richer role-level talent-market intelligence,
- market-calibration agents are already entering recruiting workflows,
- done-for-you source-verified market maps are already commercially mature,
- public-data talent-intelligence workflows are increasingly inexpensive to reproduce,
- CareerOneStop commercial transformation rights require additional clarification.

The project did **not** implement the paid Role Reality endpoint.
